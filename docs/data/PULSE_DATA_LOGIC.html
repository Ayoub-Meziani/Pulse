How the data reaches PULSE

  • Cuff, direct Bluetooth: phone browser with Web Bluetooth (Chrome / Edge on Android or desktop; not Safari / iPhone) → FastAPI → measurements.
  • Any device with a CSV export: export file → our import tool (python -m pulse_data.ingest import-csv) BUILT. It checks columns and units, rejects bad rows with reasons and refuses the same file twice.
  • Ring / bracelet vendor cloud or SDK: depends on the brand. Many need a developer account or partnership. Choose the device first, then check its export options.

Rules for ring / bracelet data

  • Never show a ring estimate as a measured blood pressure. Always label it "estimate".
  • Estimates are needs_review, so they are left out of averages, trends and ML training (team rule: valid readings only).
  • A high estimate should ask the patient for a cuff reading. It should not trigger the emergency path on its own; symptoms always can. clinician to confirm
  • Heart rate / HRV / SpO2 / skin temperature go to wearable_samples (patient, device, metric, value, unit, time, source). Written by the backend only; demo data has a simulated ring for Karim, Nadia and Fatima. LIVE
Recommendation for the demo: a validated upper-arm cuff gives real numbers that the database already handles. A ring is good for activity and sleep context, not for the blood-pressure value itself.

6. Do the fields match the Algerian factors that affect blood pressure?

What the Algerian national survey shows (WHO STEPwise 2016–2017, analysis by Moussouni et al., BMC Public Health 2022; 6,765 adults aged 18–69):
  • Hypertension 31.6%, prehypertension 36.2%. Hypertension is higher in women (34.2%) than men (28.3%).
  • 41.1% of people with hypertension did not know they had it.
  • Independent risk factors for hypertension: age (60–69 vs 18–29: odds ratio 12.3), hypercholesterolemia (2.0), obesity BMI ≥ 30 (1.7), abdominal obesity (1.7), separated / divorced (2.9).
  • Common but not significant after adjustment: daily smoking 11.1%, physical inactivity 19.4%, diabetes / high blood sugar 17.5%. Obesity 24.9%, overweight 35.0%, abdominal obesity 67.8%.
Salt: bread is described as a major salt source in western Algeria (low-salt bread study). A pilot study in Casablanca bakeries (Morocco) found about 17 g of salt per kg of bread, roughly 8–9 g of salt a day from bread alone. We found no measured national salt-intake figure for Algeria.
Ramadan: small studies show blood pressure mostly unchanged when treatment continues. Medication timing (iftar or suhoor) varies, and fasting brings a risk of dehydration. Any change of timing or dose is the doctor's decision only.

7. The full logic

7.1 Life of one reading

1. EnterPatient types it (or device / CSV)
2. Format checkFastAPI / importer: numbers, unit mmHg, time
3. ValidateDB trigger pulse-v1 sets valid / needs_review / invalid + flags
4. RulesBackend rules evaluate reading + symptoms
5. AlertCreate or attach to an open alert (no duplicates)
6. ShowNext step for the patient; emergency screen if red flags
7. DoctorPatient list sorted: emergency > severe > trend > notice
8. MLBatch job: next-day estimate for the doctor

7.2 Validation policy pulse-v1 (database trigger, applies to every reading)

ResultRule
invalid (stored, not used)systolic ≤ diastolic · systolic outside 50–300 · diastolic outside 25–200 · pulse outside 25–250 · more than 5 minutes in the future
needs_review (stored, shown with flags)systolic outside 70–250 or diastolic outside 40–150 · estimated (not a cuff) · same values within 2 minutes (double entry) · device reported a problem
valideverything else. A very high reading (e.g. 186/118) is valid data; it is an alert matter, not a data problem.

Open point: the backend's own Python validation uses diastolic ≥ 30 and a pulse-pressure check (20–120). The team contract makes the database trigger the single source of truth. Whether to add a pulse-pressure flag to the trigger is a clinician question.

7.3 Alert rules (backend rules.py, deterministic, independent of ML)

RuleWhenLevelWhat happens
R-EMG-1Any red-flag symptom: chest_pain, shortness_of_breath, back_pain, numbness, weakness, vision_change, difficulty_speakingemergencyEmergency screen immediately, whatever the reading. Raises a recent severe alert instead of making a second one.
R-SEV-1systolic ≥ 180 or diastolic ≥ 120severeSymptom check, then recheck after 60 seconds
R-SEV-2Recheck within 15 min still ≥ 180 / ≥ 120severe, persistentAttached to the same alert; urgent guidance
R-SEV-3Recheck within 15 min below 180 / 120severeAlert stays open for the doctor; reading attached
R-TGT-1Reading ≥ target (care plan, default 140/90)noticeOne active notice per patient
R-TRD-1≥ 3 valid readings in 7 days and their mean ≥ targettrendOne active trend alert per patient

Alert states: open → acknowledged (patient or doctor) → reviewed (doctor) → resolved (doctor; a note is required for severe / emergency). Expected mapping to the database alert_class: emergency → possible_emergency, severe → severely_high, notice → above_target, trend → above_target_trend confirm with backend.

7.4 Summary numbers (backend summary.py)

  • 7-day and 30-day average systolic / diastolic, 7-day maximum, % of readings above target, days with a reading. Valid readings only. needs_review and invalid readings are counted, not averaged.
  • Trend: last 7 days vs the 7 days before, at least 3 readings in each. Systolic change ≥ +5 → rising; ≤ −5 → falling; else stable; otherwise "insufficient data".

7.5 ML model (next task, data/AI)

ItemDecision (team contract §4)
PredictsNext day's morning mean systolic and diastolic (readings before 12:00 local time)
InputsValid readings from before the target day only; blood-pressure history + day of week (no lifestyle in the MVP)
Eligibility≥ 6 valid readings on ≥ 4 days in the last 7 days and ≥ 1 morning reading; otherwise status "insufficient_history"
Training dataSynthetic set pulse-sim-v1 (200 patients × 90 days). Labelled synthetic everywhere
TestingPatients split 70 / 15 / 15 (no patient in two sets), time order kept. Compared with two baselines: yesterday's morning value and the 7-day mean
ModelsRegularised linear regression and gradient boosting; keep the best on validation, test once
MetricsMAE and RMSE in mmHg with 95% confidence intervals over patients
Outputml_predictions, doctor-only panel "Model estimate (synthetic training data)"
NeverWrites alerts or measurements, decides an emergency, or suggests medication changes

7.7 ML phase 2: live ring, weekly doctor loop, classification (built 30 Oct, local)

1. RingSamples arrive in wearable_samples (simulated stream for now)
2. MonitorLive 10-minute and night windows; compared with medical references, the personal normal (28 nights) or the doctor's band
3. Flagswearable_flags: advisory, never alerts, never the safety path
4. WeeklyReport per patient: home-BP category, control vs target, P(next week above target), ring summary, proposed bands
5. DoctorApproves or edits bands (approve_band), confirms or dismisses flags, reviews the report
6. LearnDoctor labels retune personal thresholds only; recall gate; no automatic deployment

Safety: the ring rule R-RING-1 (SpO2 below 94 % over 10 minutes → prompt a cuff reading + symptom check) is specified for the backend to run directly on raw samples, so the safety path never depends on an ML job. Home-BP categories use the ACC/AHA 2017 home values (135/85 = office 140/90), never office grades.

7.6 Safety rules that hold everywhere

  • No diagnosis, no prescription, no advice to start / stop / change medication.
  • Symptoms trigger the emergency path on their own; ML never delays urgent guidance.
  • Nothing is silently deleted: wrong readings stay, flagged. Readings cannot be edited by app users.
  • Synthetic data is always labelled; ring estimates are always labelled.
  • Health values never go into logs or error messages.
